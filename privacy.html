Atlas95 - Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Atlas95 is a free study game. This page explains what information it uses and why. The short version: your progress stays in your browser, and the only things shared with other players are the nickname you pick and your game scores.

What stays on your device

Your progress, best scores, settings, streaks and lesson history are saved in your browser's local storage. They aren't sent to us. Clearing your browser data erases them.

What's sent to our leaderboard

To show the shared leaderboard, the game creates an anonymous account for your browser. No email or password is needed. It saves:

  • A random account ID
  • The leaderboard name you choose (please don't use your full real name)
  • Game stats: XP, level, streaks, badges count, answer totals, and your latest daily challenge result

Anyone using Atlas95 can see leaderboard names and scores. This data is stored with our database provider, Supabase.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, location or contacts.

Services that handle requests

Like most websites, the services that deliver Atlas95 can see basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type when you load the page. These include our host (Vercel), our database (Supabase) and the public code libraries the page loads (jsDelivr and cdnjs). Their own privacy policies apply. Pronunciation audio uses your device's built-in voices and isn't sent to us.

Removing your data

Open Scoreboard > Settings and choose Remove me from the leaderboard to delete your leaderboard entry. To erase your progress, clear this site's data in your browser. You can also contact us and we'll delete your entry.

Children

Atlas95 isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Some language lessons include casual swear words. If you believe a child under 13 has posted information to the leaderboard, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
